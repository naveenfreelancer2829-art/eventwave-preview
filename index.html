FAQ

AV rental in Bangalore, answered.

Quick answers about renting sound systems, LED walls, lighting and projectors from Event Wave.

What equipment can I rent from Event Wave in Bangalore?+

Sound systems and line-array packages (JBL, RCF, QSC), audio mixers, stage and effect lighting, P3 and P2 LED walls, projectors, 43" to 75" TVs and touch screens, AV accessories, trussing and special effects such as smoke, haze, cold fire and confetti blasters.

Are the prices per day?+

Yes. All catalogue prices are per day, except the P2 LED wall, which is priced per square foot. Transportation is charged separately.

Do you deliver and set up the equipment?+

Yes. We deliver to your venue, set up the equipment and pick it up after the event. Every unit is quality-checked before dispatch. Projectors can also be collected from our office, with delivery charged extra.

Which events do you provide AV setups for?+

Corporate events, conferences, product launches, weddings and receptions, concerts and exhibitions, for any audience size and budget.
